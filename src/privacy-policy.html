Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Accretion Power (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share personal information when you visit our website, create an account, access product resources, request a quote, or contact us.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when interacting with our website or communicating with us. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, company name, and job title.
  • Account registration information.
  • Product selections, quantities, specifications, and project details submitted with quote requests.
  • Messages, documents, and other information you choose to share.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we and our service providers may collect technical and usage information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring website, pages visited, and interactions with website features.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to questions and product inquiries.
  • Prepare quotes and discuss your project requirements.
  • Review account registrations and manage access to restricted resources.
  • Provide datasheets and technical information.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website.
  • Understand website usage and improve the visitor experience.
  • Protect accounts, prevent misuse, and maintain website security.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Account registrations and quote requests may be forwarded to authorized Accretion Power personnel for review and follow-up.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support account sign-in, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use the website.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect account access or other website features. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using nonessential cookies or similar technologies.

How We Share Information

We may share personal information with service providers that help us operate our website and business, including providers of website hosting, account authentication, email delivery, analytics, and data storage.

We may also disclose information when required by law, in response to lawful requests, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Accretion Power, our customers, or others.

If Accretion Power is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including managing accounts, responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information, the nature of our relationship with you, and applicable business and legal requirements.

Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to request closure of your account.

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have additional privacy rights. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Certain information may need to be retained for legal obligations or other permitted purposes.

You may choose not to provide personal information. However, this may limit our ability to provide account access, prepare a quote, or respond to your inquiry.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or resources. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those websites, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, business practices, or legal obligations. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We will provide additional notice when required by applicable law.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to submit a privacy request, please contact Accretion Power through the contact form on our website. For privacy requests, include “Privacy Request” in your message so we can direct your inquiry appropriately.
