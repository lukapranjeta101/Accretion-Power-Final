# Accretion Power business information

Extracted October 28, 2026 from the existing working tree. Statements below reflect the website’s claims, with source traceability. No appearance-based project, ownership, location, or technical claims are added.

## Identity and purpose

Accretion Power, LLC is an electrical component supplier combining electrical products, manufacturing expertise, and personal service for North American utilities and global original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). It is based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, United States. The stated focus is helping customers build with confidence and connecting component requirements to appropriate manufacturing capabilities.

“Accretion” is explained as growth through the addition of smaller parts. The company applies that idea to partnerships: components, conversations, and projects add up. No founding year or detailed corporate history is published in the current source.

## Manufacturing, sourcing, and value propositions

- Its own epoxy manufacturing facility in India, with in-house tooling for custom designs.
- A wider manufacturer network for porcelain, composite, and copper components.
- Locally managed inventory in Pennsylvania, described as supporting shorter lead times and reducing purchasing workload; no quantified delivery commitment is stated.
- Custom copper cutting, milling, punching, and bending; C110 copper bus bar with bare, tin-plated, or silver-plated finishes.
- Direct communication with Ben Vietmeier and a team with switchgear experience.
- Help starting from a standard part, drawing, application requirement, or replacement component; discussion of materials, dimensions, mounting details, quantities, and delivery needs.

## Products

- Epoxy: low- and medium-voltage post insulators; resistive/capacitive voltage detecting insulators; terminal, oil seal, transformer, primary roof, RMU, and three-phase bushings; phase spouts; other epoxy components. Homepage also mentions thru and wall bushings.
- Porcelain: transmission and distribution insulators, including hollow/solid core, line post, long rod/disc, railway, standoff, spool, guy strain, transformer bushings, pin, suspension, and wall bushings.
- Composite: solid core post, line post, long rod (AC/DC per catalog), and railway/metro insulators; outdoor applications.
- Copper: bare, silver-plated, tin-plated, and insulated bus bar; standard 12-foot lengths are mentioned as coming from a product presentation, with dimensions to be confirmed.
- Bus boots: a published switchgear category; detailed specifications and options are not supplied.
- Castings: OEM-specific A356-T6 aluminum castings using Gravity Die Casting and Low Pressure Die Casting. Named examples include flanges for switchgear/in-tank tap changers, interrupter mounts, and front/end covers. Homepage also describes current-carrying and pressure-retaining designs to OEM requirements.
- Supplemental catalog/portal entries: GPO-3 fiberglass insulation barriers and cold-wound springs for trip, close, and wipe applications. Not listed among the current six public product families; confirm intended marketing scope.
